01 / profile

Engineering with a bias toward the real world.

Hi, I'm Samuel Smith — a mechanical engineering student / engineer focused on turning ideas into hardware that can be modeled, manufactured, and tested. I enjoy the point where design decisions meet physical constraints: loads, materials, tolerances, packaging, cost, and manufacturability.

My portfolio is built around the engineering process, not just final renders. For each project, I aim to show the problem, design decisions, analysis, prototype, and evidence behind the result.

I'm currently looking for mechanical engineering internships and early-career opportunities where I can contribute to product design, manufacturing, robotics, test, or other hands-on engineering work.
